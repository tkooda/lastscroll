# LastScroll Privacy Policy

LastScroll collects no personal data.

- The only data it keeps is your settings (timer minutes and the list of
  selected apps), stored locally on your device. They are not backed up.
- Accessibility events (taps, scrolls, window changes, volume key presses)
  are used only in memory to reset or pause the sleep timer while it runs.
  They are not stored, logged to any server, or shared.
- The app has no network access (no INTERNET permission) and sends nothing
  off the device.
- No analytics, ads, or third-party libraries.
