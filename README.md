# LastScroll

A sleep timer for short-video apps.

Ever fallen asleep scrolling short videos? The phone keeps playing all
night, the screen stays on and the battery is empty by morning.

LastScroll watches for that. While its timer runs, every tap, scroll or
volume button press in the apps you choose starts the countdown over. When
you stop touching the phone for the minutes you picked, LastScroll pauses
the video and turns the screen off.

## How it can help

- No more videos playing until morning.
- A phone that still has battery when you wake up.
- A quiet room once you have drifted off.

## How to use

1. Open LastScroll and pick the apps you watch videos in.
2. Set how many minutes of no touching should count as asleep.
3. Start the timer from the app or from its home-screen shortcut.
4. To stop it, tap Cancel on the LastScroll notification.

If Android will not let you turn on the accessibility setting, open
Settings > Apps > LastScroll, tap the three-dot menu, choose "Allow
restricted settings", then try again.

## Permissions

- Notifications: to show the countdown and a Cancel button.
- Accessibility: to notice taps and scrolls, only in the apps you chose,
  and to turn the screen off when time is up.

LastScroll has no internet access and collects nothing. See
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
